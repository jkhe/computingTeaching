Quick Check

Show me on your fingers: how many readings do we take at each location?

Crucial Rule

Why take 3 readings?

Imagine you are measuring a quiet garden. Right as you press the button to record... a dog barks loudly!

If you only took one reading, your data would say the garden is loud. Taking three readings helps us spot sudden mistakes (anomalies) so our data is reliable.

The micro:bit Sensor

Watch the micro:bit — press Button A — a number appears. That number is the sound level. 0 = silence. 255 = very loud. You will read that number and write it on your sheet at the park.
